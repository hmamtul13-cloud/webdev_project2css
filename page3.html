My First Trip to New York

One of the most memorable experiences I have had was taking my first road trip to New York. I had always wanted to visit New York because of so many movies I had watched and how much everyone seemed to love it. I was able to experience the busy streets of Times Square, visit the Statue of Liberty, ride in the famous NYC yellow taxi, and try the beloved food from Halal Guys.

My First Impressions

When I first arrived in New York City, one of the things that stood out to me was how busy the entire city seemed. There were people constantly rushing past, cars and taxis honking, and buildings that seemed to touch the sky. It was so exciting to finally see a place I had heard and seen so much about up close!

Places I Visited

During my trip, I had the opportunity to visit several different places throughout New York. I first visited Times Square. It was so packed, and all the advertisements practically came alive. I then visited the Statue of Liberty. We took a ferry all the way to the island to see it up close. It was incredibly surreal. I also checked out Central Park. There was so much greenery, plus lots of plants and animals. Each location truly gave me a different experience.

  • Statue of Liberty
  • Central Park

My Favorite Part of the Trip

My favorite part of the trip was visiting all of these places with my cousins. This was especially memorable to me because it was my very first trip! It is definitely an experience that I will remember forever.

Food and Experiences

Another big part of my trip that I really enjoyed was trying different foods and experiencing the culture of New York. Since I already love baking and cooking, trying food was definitely something I was looking forward to. I was able to try the NYC favorite, Halal Guys. It was my first time trying it, and it was just as good as people said. The rice was fluffy, the chicken was juicy, and the sauce was incredibly refreshing.

What I Learned From My Trip

My first trip to New York definitely taught me that traveling to a new place can give you exposure to so many incredible experiences. I was able to see so many new things, experience a completely different environment, and make incredible memories with my family.

Would I Visit Again?

I would definitely love to visit New York again. If I had the opportunity to come back, I would like to visit with my cousins again. There are still so many places I would love to explore and experiences I would like to have.
